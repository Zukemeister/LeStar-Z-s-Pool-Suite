LeStar Z.’s
Chess Clock

Set it up, put the tablet between you, and play.

Who is playing
Time for each side

The two sides need not match. Giving the stronger player less time is the oldest handicap there is.

White moves first, so white's pad is the one that lights up. After you move, tap your own pad — that stops your clock and starts your opponent's. Space bar works too, if there is a keyboard.
