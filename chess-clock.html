LeStar Z.’s
Chess Clock

Set it up, put the tablet between you, and play.

Who is playing
Pick a colour each

A colour belongs to the PLAYER and is held all game, so the one cue you can rely on never changes. Two players cannot take the same one.

Who takes white?

White moves first, so whoever takes it has the small advantage. Choose, or let the coin decide.

Time for each player

The two need not match. Giving the stronger player less time is the oldest handicap there is.

Whoever has white moves first, and that pad is the one that lights up. After you move, tap your own pad — that stops your clock and starts your opponent's. Space bar works too, if there is a keyboard.
